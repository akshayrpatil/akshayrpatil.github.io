I'm a Senior Product Designer with six years across consumer and B2B products, and I've made peace with the fact that the interesting part is never the pretty part. It's the stretch where nobody agrees on the problem yet.

Right now I own the pre-purchase funnel and beauty tech innovation at Nykaa Beauty — the product page, the moment where browsing either becomes a confident purchase or quietly leaks away. Before that I worked on social commerce and the affiliate program at Nykaa, built the credit and disbursement experience for the Udaan Capital app, and worked end-to-end on Pickily, Udaan's quick-commerce grocery app.

I like sitting close to PMs and engineers from discovery to ship. Half of my job turns out to be making a decision legible to other people — a data cut, a research quote, a fold-by-fold teardown — and I've come to enjoy that as much as the craft itself. I care about the system behind a product as much as the pixels on the screen.
